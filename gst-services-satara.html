About GST Services in Satara

Goods & Services Tax (GST) compliance is one of the most critical ongoing obligations for any business in India. Missing a filing deadline or making an error in GSTR returns can lead to penalties, interest charges, and input tax credit (ITC) loss — directly hurting your cash flow.

CA Reshma Jadhav & Company provides end-to-end GST services in Satara — from initial registration to monthly filing, audit support, and ITC optimization. We serve traders, manufacturers, service providers, e-commerce sellers, and professionals across Satara, Karad, and Pune.

What's Included

  • GST Registration — new business GST enrollment within 3–5 working days
  • Monthly & Quarterly GSTR-1, GSTR-3B Filing — zero-penalty, on-time
  • GSTR-9 Annual Return & GST Audit for applicable businesses
  • Input Tax Credit (ITC) Review & Optimization — maximize your refunds
  • GST Notice Handling & Assessment Response
  • GST Refund Processing — export refunds & inverted duty structure
  • E-way Bill & E-invoice Setup & Compliance

Who Needs This

Mandatory GST Registration: Any business with annual turnover above ₹40 lakh (goods) or ₹20 lakh (services) must register for GST. E-commerce sellers and interstate suppliers register from day one regardless of turnover.

We serve: retail traders and wholesalers, manufacturers, service professionals (consultants, agencies, doctors), construction contractors, exporters, importers, and e-commerce sellers — any business that is GST-registered or needs to register in Satara.

Why Timely GST Filing Matters

Late GSTR filing attracts ₹50–₹200 per day in late fees plus 18% annual interest on unpaid tax. Mismatches between GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B can trigger notices and block your customers' ITC claims — damaging business relationships.

With CA Reshma Jadhav, your GST filings happen before the due date, every month — and we proactively flag ITC mismatches and reconcile your books before they become problems.
