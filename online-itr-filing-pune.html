Remote ITR Filing for Pune

You don't need a CA down the road — you need one who files correctly, claims every deduction you're entitled to, and is reachable when a notice arrives. CA Reshma Jadhav & Company is a Satara-based Chartered Accountant practice that serves Pune clients entirely remotely for income tax return filing.

Everything happens online: you share your documents over WhatsApp or a secure upload link, your return is prepared and reviewed by a qualified CA, your tax position is explained in plain language, and the return is filed — with the acknowledgement shared back to you. No office visit, no paperwork runs across the city.

Who We File For in Pune

  • Salaried employees — Form 16 returns, HRA, LTA, 80C/80D and other deductions optimised
  • IT & tech professionals — RSUs/ESOPs, multiple Form 16s on job change, foreign income
  • Freelancers & consultants — presumptive taxation (44ADA), expense claims, advance tax planning
  • Independent professionals — doctors, architects, lawyers and other practitioners
  • Small business owners & traders — business income, books support, advance tax
  • Investors — capital gains on shares, mutual funds and property

What's Included

  • ITR-1 through ITR-4 preparation and e-filing for individuals and professionals
  • Old vs new regime comparison — filed under whichever lowers your legal tax
  • Deduction & exemption review — 80C, 80D, HRA, home loan interest, and more
  • Capital gains computation — shares, mutual funds, property
  • Refund maximisation and accurate computation to avoid scrutiny
  • Income tax notice handling, rectification and assessment response
  • Revised and belated return filing
  • Advance tax computation for freelancers and business income

How It Works — 3 Simple Steps

1. Share your documents — Send PAN, Form 16 and proofs on WhatsApp or upload them securely.
2. CA review & tax discussion — Your return is prepared, both regimes are compared, and your tax position is explained.
3. Filing & acknowledgement — After your confirmation, the return is filed and the ITR-V acknowledgement is shared with you.

The whole process is built for people who want a real Chartered Accountant without the back-and-forth of in-person visits. No office visit needed for Pune clients.

Filing for a business rather than yourself? Proprietorships, partnership firms, LLPs and companies need their books finalised and a balance sheet prepared before the return can be filed — and often a tax audit too. That work is covered on the business ITR filing page, which also handles business balance sheets and business nil returns for dormant entities. Pune businesses are served remotely on the same basis.

Documents You'll Need

  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Form 16 (salaried) / income summary (freelancers, business)
  • Bank statements and interest certificates
  • Investment & deduction proofs (80C, 80D, home loan, etc.)
  • Capital gains statements (broker / mutual fund), if any
  • Details of any other income — rent, freelance receipts, foreign income
