👩‍💼 Meet CA Reshma Jadhav

CA Reshma Jadhav (FCA, M.Com, DISA) is a Satara-based Chartered Accountant with 10+ years of experience in income tax, GST, startup registration, accounting and business advisory — serving over 500 clients across Satara, Karad, Wai and Pune.

A dedicated Chartered Accountant with extensive experience in financial consulting, business advisory, and strategic planning for SMEs, startups, and individuals across Satara and Maharashtra. Known for her personal approach, tech-enabled solutions, and zero-penalty track record.

Beyond client practice, CA Reshma is a certified ICAI trainer, conducting sessions for CA students at the ICAI branches of Sangli, Ichalkaranji and Satara. This dual role — practitioner and educator — reflects her deep commitment to the CA profession and the Maharashtra business community.
